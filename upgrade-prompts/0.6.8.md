# Upgrade to devrouter 0.6.8

Managed status reports unreadable lifecycle history as drift. Newer-writer history
requires a matching or newer reader while preserving the journal; other unreadable
history requires inspection. Status and doctor prioritize that remedy, failed
transition inspection, durable operation attention and live-owner guidance before
ordinary startup or nested-mount restart advice. Ordinary readable-history drift
retains normal ensure guidance. No configuration schema, command surface,
dependency, ownership or admission guard changes are introduced.

## Task

Within approved installation/adaptation scope, select the integrity-verified exact
package, update `devrouter.version` in `.devrouter.yml` and refresh existing agent
guidance through `devrouter repo agents`. Verify selected/PATH executables and
concrete package, source, harness and controller pins sharing the affected journal
namespace or mixed caller chain. Owners confirm compatible adoption or explicit
non-use; a PATH listing alone does not prove participation. Independent remote
state is a separate adoption boundary. Unknown affected participation holds
activation. Do not alter dormant harness checks or replay consumed attempts.

A new reader accepts older supported history. Older 0.6.7 readers refuse actual
0.6.8-written journals through direct read, update, enumeration and admission even
without a schema change. Installing alone writes no lifecycle journal. Before
any separately authorized new lifecycle write, coordinate affected clients; keep
inactive older pins unused until compatibility is established. After newer writes,
use a matching or newer CLI and preserve all journal bytes and durable
initialization/replacement receipts. Never erase, downgrade or replay history to
bypass refusal or rearm an attempt.

Publication, installation and notices authorize no consumer startup, repair,
replacement, initialization, reset or extra attempt. Inspection is read-only
and does not establish application readiness. A live owner must finish or be
explicitly cancelled within its own authority before another operation.

## Validation

1. Verify registry integrity, selected package/member bytes and executable
   versions. Run native checks for actual consumer edits; preserve journals and
   receipts when only selecting a CLI.
2. Use `devrouter status --repo <checkout> --json` and existing diagnostic evidence
   within authorized inspection scope. Check the reported reason and actual
   next-command preconditions; do not infer permission from generic advice.
3. Any separately authorized runtime work ends with canonical stop and exact
   worker, provider, container and route proof unless the user keeps it running.

## Report

Report selected package/executable identities, affected-client adoption or holds,
remaining unknowns and relevant checks. Any consumer recovery claim needs its
actual useful-work, retained-data, neighbour and stopped-terminal evidence.
Synthetic qualification and package identity alone do not prove receiving-host
adoption, consumer recovery or retained-maintenance acceptance.
